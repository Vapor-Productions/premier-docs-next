# Privacy Policy

<p className="lead">How Premier Planner collects and uses data.</p>

## Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Premier Planner ("we", "us", "our") collects,
uses, and protects information when you use our Discord bot and documentation
hosted at `https://docs.premierplanner.eu`.

## Information we collect

- **Discord account data:** Discord user id, username, language preference, and team identifiers in servers where the bot is used.
- **Riot account data (only after you opt in via Riot Sign-On):** Riot `puuid`, current Riot ID (`gameName` and `tagLine`), and the time you linked.
- **Usage data:** commands used, timestamps, and feature interactions needed to operate the bot.
- **Technical data:** basic request metadata for the RSO callback and troubleshooting.

We do **not** collect or store your Riot password. Authentication happens on
Riot’s Sign-On pages.

## Opt-in and visibility (Riot / VALORANT)

Account linking is optional. When you use `/riot link` and complete Riot
Sign-On, you **opt in** to sharing your Riot identity with Premier Planner.

**By linking, your Riot ID and team membership / Premier roster status may be
visible to other members of Discord servers where you use Premier Planner.** Do
not link if you do not want that visibility.

We do not expose historic Riot IDs. We store your current Riot ID as returned by
Riot at link time.

## How we use information

- Provide team organisation, roster checks, and related Premier Planner features.
- Verify that a Discord user opted in to a specific Riot identity.
- Operate, secure, and improve the service.
- Respond to support and security issues.

## Data retention

We retain linked Riot and Discord data while your account is linked and the bot
needs it to provide features, or as required by law.

You can unlink at any time with `/riot unlink` in Discord, which clears your
stored Riot identity from Premier Planner. You can also request deletion at any
time (see Contact).

## Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers for
hosting or support under confidentiality obligations. Riot account linking uses
Riot Sign-On; Riot processes login on their systems under their policies.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No method of
transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete
your personal information, including unlinking (`/riot unlink`) or deleting
stored Riot identity data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted at
`https://docs.premierplanner.eu/legal/privacy-policy`.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at
[support@premierplanner.eu](mailto:support@premierplanner.eu).

## Overview

Premier Planner is a Discord bot for Valorant Premier team organisation. Players
who want roster verification and team features link their Riot account with
**official Riot Sign-On (RSO)**. Linking is optional and player-initiated.

Riot reviewers: this page is the public visual flow of account linking. You do
**not** need to join a Discord server to understand the product.

## What we use Riot for

| Capability | How |
| --- | --- |
| Player identity | Riot Sign-On + Account API `GET /riot/account/v1/accounts/me` |
| Stored fields | `puuid`, current Riot ID (`gameName` + `tagLine`), link timestamp, Discord user id |
| Premier roster checks | Team name/tag lookup to mark whether a linked member appears on a Premier roster |

We do **not** provide item store checkers, cheats, real-time performance
overlays, betting, or unofficial ranked ladders.

## Opt-in and public data

By linking, the player opts in to sharing their Riot identity with Premier
Planner. Their **Riot ID** and **team membership / roster status** may be
visible to members of Discord servers where they use the bot.

Full details: [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## User flow

<FlowSteps />

### Step 1 — Start in Discord

The player runs `/riot link` (available to every user; no admin required).

They receive an **ephemeral** message that includes:

1. The opt-in disclaimer (linking makes Riot identity / team status visible in that server context).
2. A link to the privacy policy.
3. A **Sign in with Riot** button.

### Step 2 — Riot Sign-On

The button opens Riot’s authorize page (`auth.riotgames.com`) with scopes
`openid` and `offline_access`. The player signs in with their Riot account.

### Step 3 — Callback

Riot redirects to our registered `RIOT_REDIRECT_URI`. The bot exchanges the
code, calls `accounts/me`, and saves the identity for that Discord user. The
success page repeats the opt-in notice.

### Step 4 — Use in Discord

After linking, Premier Planner can associate the Discord member with their Riot
identity for team and Premier roster features (for example: on-roster /
not-on-roster markers on a team view).

Unlinked players are simply shown as not linked; we do not invent or scrape
identity for players who have not opted in via RSO.

### Step 5 — Unlink (opt-out)

Players can remove their Riot identity at any time with `/riot unlink`. That
clears the stored `puuid`, Riot ID, and link timestamp for that Discord user.
Team and roster features then treat them as not linked until they run
`/riot link` again.
